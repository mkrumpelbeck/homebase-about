Homebase privacy policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Homebase is a private family organizer that runs on a computer in the owner's home. It is used only by the owner's household. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

Who can use it

Only the owner. Homebase is not offered to the public, and no one else is asked to connect a Google account.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, Homebase has read-only access to the owner's Gmail. It reads messages the owner has chosen (for example school newsletters and household reminders), including their attachments, to list upcoming dates and to-dos on the family's own screens. It cannot send, change or delete email.

Where the data goes

Email content is processed and stored on the owner's home computer. To pull dates and to-dos out of a message, its text may be sent to an AI service acting only as a processor for that purpose. Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

How long it is kept

Items are kept until the household removes them. The owner can remove Homebase's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops all reading immediately.

Google API data

Homebase's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Contact the owner through GitHub.
