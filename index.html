Homebase

A private family organizer

Homebase is a personal project: a family calendar and to-do board that runs on a computer in the owner's home. It is used only by the owner's household and is not offered to the public. There is nothing to sign up for or download.

It connects to the owner's own Google account, read-only, to show school and household reminders on the family's screens.
